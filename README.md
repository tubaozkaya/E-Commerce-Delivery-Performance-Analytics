## 🎯 Key Objectives
This analysis provides data-driven answers to the following business questions:
* **Delivery Lead Times:** What are the average shipping durations and late delivery rates?
* **Geographical Performance:** Which regions or specific routes experience the highest volume of logistical bottlenecks?
* **Courier & Carrier Efficiency:** Who are the top-performing and underperforming distribution partners?
* **Customer Satisfaction:** How do delivery delays directly correlate with customer feedback and return rates?
